# ATLAS Privacy Notice (Prototype)

Last updated: 2026-10-18.

This is a static proof of concept, not a production service.

## What data is collected?
The profile fields and saved opportunity IDs you enter are stored in your own browser's localStorage. The app does not have a server endpoint and does not intentionally send profile information to ATLAS or GitHub. Standard hosting providers may process request logs when delivering web pages.

## How to delete data
Select **Delete my data** in the app. You can also clear your browser's local site data. Clearing storage may erase your saved plan irreversibly.

## External links
Opening a third-party official website is subject to that website's own privacy policy. ATLAS does not control external content.

## Warnings
Do not enter national ID numbers, health information, bank account details, passwords or private documents in this prototype.

## Contact
Use the repository issue tracker for general feedback only. Never post personal or confidential information in public issues.

A production launch requires a reviewed privacy notice, access controls, threat modeling, retention rules and GDPR compliance assessment.
